Doc Populi · Shelf 02

Healthcare Systems.

The structures, incentives, and frictions that shape modern care.

Medicine is a human story before it is a scientific one, but it is also a system story. Reimbursement, coding, hospital service districts, telemedicine, the long arc of an EMR rollout — these are not glamorous topics, and they decide almost everything. I write here, plainly, about how the machine actually works and where it bends people who are trying to do their jobs.

What I write about

  • How billing and coding actually shape what care gets delivered.
  • Telemedicine after the pandemic — what stayed, what broke, what's next.
  • Hospital economics, service districts, and rural access.
  • The unfinished story of EMRs, FHIR, and a clinician's day.
