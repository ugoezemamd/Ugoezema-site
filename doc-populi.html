Doc Populi

Field notes from the intersection of medicine, culture, and the human future.

What this is

A notebook, kept in public.

Doc Populi explores where humanity, medicine, healthcare, wellness, and technology intersect — for a deeper understanding of who we are, where we are coming from, and where we want to go. Written by a pulmonologist who believes medicine is a human story before it is a scientific one. New essays every Sunday.

New essays every Sunday. Free to read. No paywall and no spam.
