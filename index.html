About

A physician writing the human story of medicine.

I'm a board-certified pulmonologist and critical care intensivist practicing in New Orleans. Most of my working life is spent in the ICU — at the bedside, with families, in the rooms where the science meets a person.

Outside the hospital, I write Doc Populi and host The Last Zebra Podcast. Both are attempts to take the questions I carry home and put them on the page, where you can pick them up too.
