The Podcast

The Last Zebra Podcast

Stories of medical professionals — their backgrounds, beliefs, and how they shape healthcare. The medical adage goes: "when you hear hoofbeats, think horses, not zebras." We do the opposite. We look for what's common in unique people. Heavy on the ordinary.
