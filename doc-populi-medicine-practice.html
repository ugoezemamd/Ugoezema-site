Doc Populi · Shelf 01

Medicine & Practice.

What happens between physician and patient — and what we make of it.

This is the part of medicine that does not make it into the curriculum. The room. The conversation. The moment a patient stops being a chart and starts being a person who is afraid. I write here about what the work actually feels like from the inside, and about the small craft decisions that turn an appointment into care.

What I write about

  • The texture of a fifteen-minute visit, and what gets lost in it.
  • Bedside language — the words we use, and the ones we should retire.
  • Burnout as a structural problem, not a personal failure.
  • What I learned from patients my training did not prepare me for.
